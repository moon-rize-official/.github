# 🌘 Moon-Rize

**We design, develop, and build software, websites, and plugins for DJs, kollektive, festivals, musicians, and artists.**

Moon-Rize sits at the intersection of underground rave culture and digital craft — a collective and studio turning what the scene needs into tools that actually work for it: event platforms, community infrastructure, creative tools, and the sites that hold it all together.

🔗 **[moon-rize.de](https://moon-rize.de)**

---

### What we build

- **Websites & digital products** — for the scene, and for the artists, kollektive, and festivals in it
- **Community infrastructure** — Discord, events, and the tools that keep an underground community organized
- **Plugins & tools** — small, sharp utilities for DJs, producers, and event organizers

### Where we stand

Early-stage and building in public where it makes sense. Brand identity is locked; the first products are in progress.
